# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Short Video Creator?
Short Video Creator is an AI-powered platform that helps you create professional videos automatically. It combines multiple AI technologies to generate scripts, voices, images, music, and animations.

### How does it work?
1. Enter your video topic or idea
2. Choose your preferences (style, tone, duration)
3. Our AI generates all components
4. Review and customize as needed
5. Export your finished video

### What types of videos can I create?
- Social media content
- Marketing videos
- Product demonstrations
- Educational content
- Training materials
- Promotional videos

### What formats are supported?
- Aspect ratios: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1
- Resolutions: up to 4K
- Formats: MP4, MOV, GIF
- Platforms: All major social media
