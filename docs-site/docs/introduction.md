# Welcome to Short Video Creator

Short Video Creator is an AI-powered platform that automates the creation of engaging video content. Our platform seamlessly integrates various AI services to help you create compelling videos from start to finish.

## Key Features

### Content Generation
- **AI Script Writing**: Generate engaging scripts using advanced language models
- **Voice Generation**: Convert text to natural-sounding speech
- **Image Creation**: Generate stunning visuals for your videos
- **Music Generation**: Create unique background music tracks

### Video Production
- **Animation Generation**: Bring static images to life with AI animations
- **Automated Assembly**: Combine all elements into a polished final video
- **Quality Control**: Ensure consistent quality across all video elements

### Platform Features
- **User Authentication**: Secure access with email or social login
- **Project Management**: Organize and track your video projects
- **Asset Library**: Store and reuse your generated assets
- **Export Options**: Download videos in various formats
