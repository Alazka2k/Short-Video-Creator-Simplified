# Getting Started

Welcome to Short Video Creator! This guide will help you create your first AI-powered video in minutes.

## Quick Start

1. **Create an Account**
   - Visit [Short Video Creator](https://short-video-creator.com)
   - Click "Sign Up"
   - Choose email or social login
   - Complete your profile

2. **Create Your First Project**
   - Click "New Project"
   - Name your project
   - Choose a category (optional)
   - Set project preferences

3. **Generate Your First Video**
   - Enter your video topic or idea
   - Choose content preferences
   - Click "Generate"
   - Review and customize

4. **Export and Share**
   - Choose export settings
   - Download or share directly
   - Get analytics and insights
