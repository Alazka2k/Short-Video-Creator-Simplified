# Script Tone and Style:

## Vocabulary
Parameter: {{ llmGen.vocabulary }}

### Documentary Videos
- Descriptive Language:
Use vivid adjectives and adverbs to paint a picture (e.g., "lush landscapes," "gripping stories").
- Informative Tone:
Focus on clarity and factual information (e.g., "According to recent studies...").
- Engaging Questions:
Pose rhetorical questions to provoke thought (e.g., "What drives human curiosity?").

### Educational/Instructional Videos
- Clear and Concise Language:
Use straightforward vocabulary (e.g., "Let's explore," "In this lesson, we will...").
- Technical Terms Explained:
Introduce jargon but provide definitions (e.g., "Photosynthesis is the process by which...").
- Encouraging Phrases:
Use motivational language (e.g., "You can do this!" or "Let's dive deeper!").

### Promotional Videos
- Persuasive Language:
Use compelling adjectives and action verbs (e.g., "Transform your life with...," "Discover the benefits of...").
- Call to Action:
Direct phrases urging immediate response (e.g., "Sign up today!" or "Don't miss out!").
- Positive Tone:
Emphasize benefits and positive outcomes (e.g., "Join our community of satisfied customers!").

### Narrative Films
Character-Specific Vocabulary:
Tailor language to reflect character backgrounds and personalities (e.g., formal language for a noble character vs. slang for a streetwise character).
Emotional Expression:
Use expressive language to convey feelings (e.g., "I can't believe this is happening!").
Imagery and Metaphors:
Employ figurative language to enhance storytelling (e.g., "Her smile was like sunshine on a rainy day.").

### Corporate/Business Videos
- Professional Tone:
Use formal vocabulary suitable for a corporate audience (e.g., "We aim to enhance productivity through...").
- Industry Jargon:
Incorporate relevant terminology that resonates with the target audience (e.g., "synergy," "ROI").
- Clear Objectives:
State goals clearly (e.g., "Our mission is to..." or "We strive to achieve...").

### Vlogs/Personal Videos
- Conversational Language:
Use informal, relatable vocabulary (e.g., "Hey everyone!" or "I just wanted to share...").
- Personal Anecdotes:
Include personal stories or experiences for relatability (e.g., "The other day, I realized...").
- Engagement Prompts:
Encourage interaction with viewers (e.g., "Let me know what you think in the comments!").
