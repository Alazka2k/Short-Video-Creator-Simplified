# Authentication User Guide

This guide explains how to use the authentication features of the Short Video Creator platform.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

You can create an account in two ways:

1. **Using Email and Password**
   - Click "Create Account" on the login page
   - Enter your email address
   - Choose a secure password
   - Complete the registration process
   - Verify your email address

2. **Using Google Sign-In**
   - Click "Sign in with Google"
   - Select your Google account
   - Grant necessary permissions
   - Your account will be created automatically

## Signing In

### Email Sign-In
1. Visit the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Sign In"

### Google Sign-In
1. Click "Sign in with Google"
2. Select your Google account
3. You'll be signed in automatically

## Managing Your Session

### Staying Signed In
- Your session remains active for extended periods while using the platform
- You can safely close and reopen your browser
- The platform will remember your session securely

### Signing Out

You can sign out in two ways:

1. **Single Device Logout**
   - Click your profile picture
   - Select "Sign Out"
   - Only the current device will be signed out

2. **All Devices Logout**
   - Click your profile picture
   - Select "Sign Out from All Devices"
   - All active sessions will be terminated

## Profile Management

### Viewing Your Profile
1. Click your profile picture
2. Select "Profile"
3. View your profile information

### Profile Information
Your profile includes:
- Email address
- Profile picture (from Google if using Google Sign-In)
- Account creation date
- Last login information

## Security Best Practices

### Password Guidelines
- Use a strong, unique password
- Combine uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include numbers and special characters
- Never share your password

### Account Security
- Sign out when using shared devices
- Keep your email account secure
- Monitor your active sessions
- Report suspicious activity

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

1. **Can't Sign In**
   - Verify your email address is correct
   - Check your password
   - Ensure caps lock is off
   - Try the "Forgot Password" option (coming soon)

2. **Google Sign-In Issues**
   - Ensure you're using the correct Google account
   - Check your internet connection
   - Clear browser cache if needed
   - Try a different browser

3. **Session Issues**
   - Clear browser cookies
   - Try signing out and back in
   - Check your internet connection
   - Contact support if issues persist

### Getting Help

If you encounter any issues:
1. Check this documentation
2. Review the troubleshooting steps
3. Contact our support team
