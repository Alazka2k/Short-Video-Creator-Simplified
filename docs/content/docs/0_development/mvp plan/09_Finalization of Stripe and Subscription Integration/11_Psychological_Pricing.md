# Psychological Pricing Optimization for Narravid - Updated Recommendations

## Current Status Assessment

### Already Implemented:
- **Charm pricing** (.99 endings)
- **Tiered anchoring** (Professional tier as high anchor)
- **Basic social proof** ("Most Popular" badge on Creator Tier)
- **Loss aversion** (annual discount framing)

### Key Insight from Analysis:
Creator Tier is your optimal profit target (43-51% margins), not Basic Tier. All psychological elements should guide users toward this tier.

## High-Impact Psychological Principles for Pricing Pages

### 1. Enhanced Decoy Effect (Critical Implementation)

**Current Issue:** Your pricing progression is too linear, making Creator Tier feel expensive rather than valuable.

**Solution:** Make Basic Tier feel intentionally limited to enhance Creator Tier's perceived value.

**Implementation:**
```
Basic Tier modifications:
- Emphasize "45 videos per month limit" vs Creator's "Unlimited"
- Highlight "13 scenes maximum" vs Creator's "20 scenes"
- Show "Community support only" vs Creator's "Priority email support"

Creator Tier enhancements:
- Lead with "Unlimited video production" 
- Emphasize "67% of successful creators choose this plan"
- Position as "Everything you need to succeed"
```

### 2. Visual Hierarchy & CTA Psychology

**Current Problem:** All CTA buttons look identical, providing no guidance toward your preferred conversion (Creator Tier).

**Solution:** Create clear visual hierarchy that guides users to Creator Tier.

**Implementation:**
```
Creator Tier CTA:
- Larger button size (120% of others)
- Brighter color (purple gradient vs dark solid)
- Different text: "Join 15K+ Creators" instead of "Get Started"
- Subtle glow or shadow effect

Other tier CTAs:
- Standard dark buttons
- Generic "Get Started" text
- No special effects
```

### 3. Scarcity & Urgency (Pricing Page Appropriate)

**Missing Opportunity:** No time pressure creates no urgency to decide.

**Pricing Page Appropriate Elements:**
- **Annual discount timer:** "20% off annual plans - 6 days remaining"
- **Limited availability:** "Only 23 Creator spots left this month"
- **Price increase warning:** "Price increases to €44.99 on March 1st"

**Implementation Location:** Top banner or within Creator Tier card.

### 4. Loss Aversion in Feature Presentation

**Current Approach:** Lists what you get
**Psychological Approach:** Emphasize what you lose without upgrading

**Implementation:**
```
Basic Tier:
- "Limited to 45 videos per month" (loss framing)
- "Stuck with 13-scene maximum" (limitation emphasis)

Creator Tier:
- "Never hit video limits again" (freedom framing)
- "Create unlimited long-form content" (possibility focus)
```

### 5. Cognitive Load Reduction

**Current Issue:** Too many features listed equally makes comparison difficult.

**Solution:** Highlight only the 2-3 most important features per tier (as you've already identified).

**Implementation:**
Use visual highlighting (bold, color, or background) for:
- Free: AI script generation, Professional voiceovers, Export & download
- Basic: AI video generation, No watermarks, YouTube Shorts optimization
- Creator: Extended scene support, Unlimited production, Priority support
- Professional: Priority processing, Direct Upload

### 6. Price Anchoring Enhancement

**Current:** Standard pricing display
**Enhanced:** Strategic price presentation

**Implementation:**
```
Creator Tier display:
€47.99 €39.99/month (crossed out higher price)
"Save €96 per year with annual billing"
"Most creators save €576 annually by choosing this plan"
```

### 7. Social Proof Specific to Pricing

**Appropriate for pricing page:**
- **"67% of users choose Creator Tier"**
- **"Most upgraded users choose Creator within 30 days"**
- **"Rated best value by 1,247 creators"**

**Not appropriate for pricing page:**
- Generic testimonials
- Company logos
- Feature-specific reviews

### 8. Risk Reversal for Purchase Hesitation

**Implementation:**
Add subtle risk reducers that don't clutter the page:
- **"30-day money-back guarantee"** under each CTA
- **"Cancel anytime"** in small text
- **"No setup fees"** where relevant

### 9. Progressive Disclosure

**Current:** All features shown equally
**Improved:** Show progression path clearly

**Implementation:**
```
Visual progression indicators:
Free → Basic → Creator (You are here) → Professional

Add subtle arrows between tiers showing natural upgrade path
Include text: "Most users upgrade to Creator within 30 days"
```

## Elements That DON'T Belong on Pricing Pages

Based on our analysis, these should be moved to other pages:

**Move to Homepage/Product Pages:**
- Detailed social proof testimonials
- Trust signals about security/reliability
- Company logos and press mentions
- Detailed feature explanations

**Move to Checkout Page:**
- Payment security badges
- Final testimonials
- Contact support options

**Remove Entirely:**
- Redundant call-to-action sections
- Generic trust elements
- Decorative content
