## FAQ Section

### How do tokens work?
Tokens are consumed based on the content types you create in one content creation run:
- **Script generation:** 5 tokens per video
- **Images:** 10 tokens per scene
- **Voiceovers:** 5 tokens per scene
- **Background music:** 20 tokens per video
- **Video assembly:** 20 tokens per video
- **Animations:** 30 tokens per scene *(Premium feature)*
- **Video scenes:** 50 tokens per scene *(Premium feature)*

*Note: Video generation and animation are premium features available in paid plans only.*

### Can I change plans anytime?
Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time. Unused tokens from packages never expire.

### What happens to my content?
All content you create is 100% yours. No hidden licensing fees, no attribution required. Download and use your videos anywhere.

### What happens if I exceed my token limit?
Your account won't be charged automatically. You can either upgrade your plan or purchase additional token packages to continue creating or you can wait until the next monthly reset.

### Do unused monthly tokens roll over?
Yes, each monthly token allocation is rolled over if not used. Also, tokens from one-time packages never expire.

### Can I share my account with team members?
Each account is designed for individual use for now. For teams, we recommend separate accounts or contact us about enterprise options.

### How does billing work when I change plans?
Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle to ensure you don't lose paid features.
