  # Detailed Plan: Improve caching and loading of content for job

## 1. Overview

This document outlines the improvements we need to do to improve caching and loading of content for job. Currently, the preview thumbnails and the content in the job details need a long time to load. With every refresh of the page or switching between workbench and job details page, the content is loaded again.

**The Core Requirements:**
- Review current caching and loading of content for job
- Improve caching and loading of content (e.g. preview thumbnails and the content in the job details) for job
