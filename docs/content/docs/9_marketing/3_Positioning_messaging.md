# Positioning & Messaging

## Overview

This document defines the strategic product positioning and messaging framework for "Narravid". It supports unified copywriting across website, ads, emails, and onboarding.

---

## 3.1 Positioning Statement

"Narravid is the all-in-one AI storytelling engine that gives creators modular control over every part of video production—generate complete scenes, or individual assets, in any style, from a single prompt."

---

## 3.2 Core Messaging Pillars

- **Creative Freedom:** More than templates—customize every part of your story
- **Speed:** Turn ideas into videos in minutes, not hours
- **Style Diversity:** Choose from cinematic, comic, anime, and more
- **Ownership:** Export and reuse your assets anywhere—royalty free
- **AI Quality Control:** Fine-tune your AI outputs with unprecedented control

---

## 3.3 Taglines & One-Liners

- From Prompt to Production—in Any Style You Imagine
- Modular AI Storytelling for Creators
- Create Videos Without Limits
- Prompt It. Produce It. Publish It.
- Your Vision, AI Precision
