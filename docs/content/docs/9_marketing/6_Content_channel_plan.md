# Content & Channel Plan

## Overview

This document defines the content strategy and channel priorities for Narravid—from prelaunch waitlist to launch and beyond.

---

## 5.1 Content Pillars

- **Education:** How-to guides, video demos, onboarding tips
- **Inspiration:** User stories, behind-the-scenes, showcase videos
- **Product Updates:** Feature launches, changelogs, roadmaps
- **Engagement:** Surveys, contests, early access campaigns
- **AI Explainers:** Demystifying AI concepts, responsible AI use, technology insights
- **Comparisons:** Honest evaluations vs. traditional methods and competing tools

## 5.4 Waitlist Strategy

### Goals
- Build an engaged audience before launch
- Gather early feedback on key features
- Create anticipation and FOMO

### Tactics
- **Tiered Access:** Priority access based on referrals and engagement
- **Exclusive Content:** Behind-the-scenes updates for waitlist members
- **Feature Voting:** Allow waitlist to vote on priority features
- **Countdown Communication:** Regular updates on launch timeline
- **Early-Bird Incentives:** Special pricing/features for waitlist members

### Emails
- Welcome: Explain the product vision and waitlist benefits
- Weekly Updates: Share development progress and new features
- Surveys: Gather preferences and use cases
- Exclusive Tips: Provide AI content creation advice
- Launch Announcement: Clear instructions for access

---

## 5.5 Demo Strategy

### Video Showcase Framework
- **Before/After:** Showing the transformation from idea to finished video
- **Style Variants:** Same content in different visual styles
- **Speed Demos:** Real-time creation with minimal editing
- **User Spotlights:** Featuring early adopter creations

### Distribution Channels
- Embed on landing page (autoplay without sound)
- Featured in email communications
- Social media (native uploads for each platform)
- YouTube playlist organized by use case

### Key Metrics
- View completion rate
- Signup conversion rate
- Social sharing rate
- Comment sentiment

---

## 5.6 User-Generated Content Strategy

### Incentives
- Featured creator program
- Usage credits for shared content
- Early access to new features

### Guidelines
- Provide clear sharing instructions
- Create branded hashtags
- Develop embeddable "Made with Narravid" badges

### Amplification
- Weekly showcase in email and social
- Creator spotlight interviews
- Resharing with added context

---

## 5.7 Measurement & Feedback

- Track: signups, open/click rates, video creation events, social mentions
- Adjust strategy based on analytics and user feedback
- Monitor competitor positioning and messaging
- A/B test key messages across channels

### Key Performance Indicators
- Waitlist conversion rate
- Time-to-first-creation
- Content sharing rate
- User retention at 7/30 days

*Iterate content plan as new channels prove effective or product phases evolve.*
