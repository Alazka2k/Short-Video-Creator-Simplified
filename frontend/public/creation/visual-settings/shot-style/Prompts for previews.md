# Shot Style Preview Prompts

This document contains the prompts used to generate the preview images for each shot style option in the video creation interface.

## Preview Prompts

### Preview 1
The surface of the moon, with a solitary astronaut planting a flag against the backdrop of Earth. Low-angle view, stark shadows under a bright lunar sky

### Preview 2
A historic moment at the Berlin Wall, with joyous crowds celebrating its fall under a night sky lit by fireworks. Eye-level view, capturing the emotion and energy of the scene

### Preview 3
The Great Pyramids of Giza standing tall against a vibrant sunset sky, their shadows stretching across the desert sands. Low-angle view, evening glow highlighting the pyramidal forms

### Preview 4
Albert Einstein standing before a chalkboard, his face deep in thought, chalk poised mid-air. Soft golden hour lighting. Low-angle view to emphasize his stature as a thinker.

### Preview 5
A close-up of a steaming bowl of ramen with perfectly cooked noodles, topped with slices of tender pork and a sprinkle of green onions. Evening indoor lighting, warm tones;

### Preview 6
A close-up of a lion resting in the tall grasses, its mane catching the soft light of the setting sun. The intense gaze of the lion conveys both power and wisdom. Standard lens, evening light, highlighting the majestic presence of the lion;

### Preview OLD 5
A shadowy room where ghostly figures reach out towards the caretaker, their translucent forms filled with desperation. Low-angle view, night with a pale bluish hue

### Preview OLD 6
A haunting image of the caretaker looking at his reflection, which shows multiple ghostly faces instead of his own. High-angle view, night with a haunting blue light
